### What Ayuvo does today

- **💊 Smart medicine reminders** — patients get alerts on their phone even when
  the app is closed or the phone is locked, improving treatment adherence for
  chronic and post-discharge patients.
- **📄 Medical report storage** — a secure, private place for patients to keep all
  their lab reports and documents in one place, accessible anytime. This can
  serve as **[Hospital Name]'s digital report store** for your patients.
- **🔍 Automatic report reading (OCR + AI)** — patients upload a report photo or
  PDF and the system extracts the text and **flags abnormal values** (high/low)
  against standard reference ranges.
- **🗣️ Plain-language explanations** — complex lab results explained simply so
  patients understand their own health, with a reminder to consult their doctor.
- **📈 Health trends over time** — tracks how key values (sugar, cholesterol,
  hemoglobin, etc.) change across multiple reports.
- **🤝 Share with your doctor** — patients can securely share a report via a link
  with their physician at your hospital.
- **📅 Appointments & doctor availability** — book and manage appointments.
- **❤️ Vitals & emergency info** — record vitals and keep emergency contacts/info
  ready.
- **👨‍👩‍👧 Family accounts** — one caregiver can manage medicines and records for
  elderly parents or children.
- **🔒 Security & privacy** — encrypted storage, two-factor authentication, and an
  access audit log, so patient data stays protected.
