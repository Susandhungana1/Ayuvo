## Sending strategy (so it lands in inboxes, not spam)

1. **Never put 48 addresses in To/CC.** That exposes everyone's email and screams
   spam. Use **BCC**, or better, a **mail-merge** so each hospital gets its own
   personalized email (Gmail + the free "Yet Another Mail Merge" add-on, or
   Brevo/Mailchimp free tier).
2. **Send in small daily batches** (~10–15/day over 3–4 days). Sending 48 at once
   from a personal Gmail can trip spam limits and hurt deliverability.
3. **Personalize the hospital name** in the subject and first line every time —
   it's the single biggest factor in getting opened and replied to.
4. **Prioritize the "High" confidence + private/teaching hospitals first** (rows
   1–35). They have real staffed inboxes and are your likeliest demo partners.
5. **Verify the 1 flagged "Low" row** (Pokhara/Patan `pahs.gov.np`) before
   sending — confirm the address on the hospital's own contact page.
6. **Government hospitals with generic gmail/yahoo** addresses are real but slower
   to reply; keep expectations modest there.
7. **Follow up once** after ~5–7 days if no reply. One polite nudge, then stop.
8. **From-address:** send from a clean personal Gmail (`susandhungana090@`) — do
   NOT send from the app's domain until it has proper SPF/DKIM set up, or it may
   land in spam.
9. **Include an easy opt-out** line if you scale up ("Reply STOP and I won't
   contact you again") — professional and keeps you on the right side of anti-spam
   norms.

## Realistic expectations
Cold outreach to hospitals typically gets a **5–15% reply rate**. From 48 emails,
expect a handful of genuine conversations — that's a success. Quality of the demo
matters more than list size.
